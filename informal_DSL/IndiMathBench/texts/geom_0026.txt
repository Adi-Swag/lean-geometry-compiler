Let n ≥ 4 be a natural number. Let A1A2 ··· An be a regular polygon and X = {1, 2, ..., n}. A subset {i1, i2, ..., ik} of X, with k ≥ 3 and i1 < i2 < ··· < ik, is called a good subset if the angles of the polygon Ai1Ai2 ··· Aik, when arranged in the increasing order, are in an arithmetic progression. If n is a prime, show that a proper good subset of X contains exactly four elements.
